OUR STORY

Two best friends.
Two dudes that care.

100 Dudes That Care started with two great dudes and two best friends: Kenny Stoddart and Louis Zitiello.

Both residents of the Jacksonville–Ponte Vedra community and local businessmen, Kenny and Louis share a simple goal: to do their best to give back to the community in the most meaningful way possible.

They started 100 Dudes That Care to bring neighbors together, support local causes, and turn a shared commitment to giving back into a bigger impact. At heart, they’re two dudes that care.

SMALL COMMITMENT. SHARED IMPACT.

Four times a year.
Three simple steps.

Our proposed giving model:
$100 a quarter. About an hour together.

◎

Show up.

Get together with men who care about where they live. Members nominate local nonprofits for consideration.

◈

Hear them out.

Learn from three nominated organizations. Hear what they do, ask questions, and vote for the cause to support.

✳

Make it count.

Each member gives $100 directly to the selected nonprofit. Together, 100 gifts can become $10,000 of local support.

GOOD QUESTIONS

A few things
to know.

Still curious?

Who is this group for?

Men who want to make a positive local difference, meet their neighbors, and give consistently. You don’t need fundraising experience or a big network to take part.

What’s the commitment?

The proposed model is $100 per quarterly meeting, or $400 per year, plus about an hour at each gathering. Final membership arrangements will be shared before you join.

Where does my contribution go?

The plan is for contributions to go directly to the nonprofit selected by members. No payment system is connected to this preview, and this site does not collect donations.

Which nonprofits can be nominated?

We plan to consider registered local nonprofits with a clear community benefit. The launch team will confirm the service area, eligibility rules, and review process before accepting official nominations.

Can I come along before committing?

Absolutely. Register your interest and ask about attending the launch gathering. You can learn about the idea and meet the group before deciding.

What if I can’t attend a meeting?

The intended model is a quarterly giving commitment even if you miss a gathering. Attendance, voting, and contribution policies will be confirmed before launch.
